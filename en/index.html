About us

French that's lived,
not just studied

My French Lab provides tailor-made French as a Foreign Language (FLE) courses for individuals, groups, and corporate clients. Our teaching approach is communicative and intercultural, focusing on real-life communication, cultural awareness, and learner-centered progression.

We believe that a language is not learned only through rules, but especially by discovering the culture, mindset, and philosophy of its speakers. That's why we always select authentic topics connected to France and current events — themes that enrich vocabulary and build a genuine understanding of everyday French.

What matters most to us is creating a positive, motivating, and always instructive learning environment — a space where we progress with curiosity, enjoyment, and authenticity.
